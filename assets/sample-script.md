# Welcome to Aside

This script is a short tour. Press Start in the bar below and read along.

## How it works

The script travels with your pointer, so you can keep your eyes on what you are clicking. {pause 1s} Move your mouse around now. Notice how the bubble drifts behind it and settles.

## Try the controls

Hold Control to peek under the bubble. Let go, and it comes back.

Press Control, Option and the right arrow on a Mac, or Ctrl, Alt and the right arrow on Windows, to skip to the next line.

## Recording a tutorial

Write your steps the way you would say them. For example:

Open the Settings window. {pause 2s} Now click the Privacy tab on the right.

When you are ready, open a new script and write your own.
